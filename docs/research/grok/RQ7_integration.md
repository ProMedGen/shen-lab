from verified shapes**, not a journal finding. No inspected 2024–2026

## 2. Narrative flow (entry, path, payoff)

Cover grammar: **one metaphor**, one reading order.

Recommended order for this lab:

1. **Entry:** dark field, faint crescent → bud (development).
2. **Path:** eye hits the pulsatile green ring, then orange branches
   (differentiation + vascularization).
3. **Payoff:** cyan core + a short sarcomere/calcium cue (alive
   myocardium). CRISPR, ML, and cardio-oncology stay off the focal plane
   or as a thin process strip.

Light: single key from upper left or a shallow-DOF rim on the orange
plexus. Scale progression: ~30 µm seed (background memory) → ~300 µm
doughnut (focal) → optional 4–40 µm lumen close-up.

Six equal satellites fail because there is **no entry point**. Overlap
and depth (foreground organoid, mid-ground polarity, background timeline)
fix it. Isolated icons do not.

## 3. Depth and vividness

Verified mechanics to steal:

- Sequential planes in prompts (background → foreground → focal object).
- Named camera, lens, f/1.8 shallow DOF.
- Real z-depth in the biology (~30 µm → ~300 µm, lumina 4–40 µm).
- One narrative metaphor on covers.

Not verified: a numbered inventory of atmospheric-perspective recipes
beyond those vendor and biology facts.

## 4. Color

Reporter colors that already exist in the lab’s Science 2025 figure:
**green / orange / cyan-blue** on a dark field. That avoids the Cell Stem
Cell **red+green** cover ban.

Do not:

- Recenter a red anatomical heart as the brand accent inside a green ring.
- Use OCT4-green + SOX2-red on the hero.
- Invent a six-hue satellite system; the packet has no verified six-hue

Navy field + orange plexus as the accent is compatible with “deep navy +
red accent” **only if red is not paired with the green ring**. Prefer
orange/gold as the accent on the hero.

## 5. Three geometric blueprints (ranked)

Dark 16:9. Must remain readable if the frame is later used small. The
rejected CSS claim means: do not assume `object-fit:cover` will save a
busy schematic. Put identity in the **center third**.

### 1 — Concentric doughnut (use this)

- **Center (~60% of frame width):** vascularized cardiac organoid,
  shallow DOF, low angle.
  - Inner pulsatile **green TNNT2 ring**.
  - **Orange** endothelial radiation; lumenized branches 4–40 µm
    penetrating myocardium.
  - **Cyan/blue** central SMCs and mural wrap.
  - Stacked depth from a flat ~30 µm circular micropattern to ~300 µm z.
- **Background plane (upper left, ~20% opacity):** anterior **crescent →
  beating bud** beside a gut-like tube, thin CD31 sheet between; FHF
  anterior, SHF behind.
- **Near-edge inset (optional, small):** cTnT cross-striations plus a
  calcium-wave / displacement tick. Not a heart icon.
- **Light:** rim on the orange plexus; dark navy void around.
- **Why it wins:** only geometry that is simultaneously iPSC seed,
  morphogenesis, and vascularized myocardium. CRISPR/ML/cardio-oncology
  can wait.

### 2 — Split dark/light token field plus silicon cell

- **Left half, dark:** oval cell tokens with square cores in a lattice.
- **Right half, lighter:** one biological cell on circuit traces.
- **Action:** a hand or pointer selects one token; that token expands into
  doughnut #1.
- **Relative size:** selected token / organoid is the largest object;
  lattice is texture, not a UMAP.
- **Use:** virtual-cell / multiomic reading of the same biology, not a
  substitute for #1.

### 3 — Closed pentagon + three-beat care bar (secondary strip only)

- **Right third:** five-node cycle, predict → explain → agents design →
  wet lab → update.
- **Mid strip:** left-to-right CRISPRi/a double arm (KRAB vs VPR) feeding
  a surviving-cell stream into NGS barcodes.
- **Bottom ~8%:** cardioprotection → surveillance → permissive-toxicity
  balance.
- **Do not** put this in the hero focal plane. Process, not identity.

**Mobile / 400 px:** keep blueprint 1’s doughnut in the center third so a
tight crop still shows green ring + orange branches + cyan core. Do not
rely on peripheral labels.
