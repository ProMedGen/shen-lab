# RQ1 — Visual language of iPSC biology and cardiac differentiation

**Status: Partial.** Drawable geometry is verified. A complete ten-item
open-image catalog was not independently verified for every candidate.

## 1. iPSC identity (canonical elements)

| Element | Shape / proportion | Typical colors in inspected figures | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Colony morphology | Tightly packed, **flat** ES-like colonies with defined edges | Phase-contrast greys; nuclei large, cytoplasm scant | Takahashi et al., Cell 2007: “tightly packed and flat colonies”; each cell “large nuclei and scant cytoplasm.” Not a glowing blob. |
| Micropattern seed | Circular confluent disc, millimetre-scale (2 / 4 / 6 mm in Abilez Fig. 1C) | OCT4 green, SOX2 red, DAPI blue | Experiment-specific reporter palette. Conflicts with Cell Stem Cell cover ban on red+green. Do not treat as a website brand palette. |
| Reprogramming | Somatic cell → OSKM → iPSC that can become any cell type | Simple process illustration, not a landscape | Karagiannis / Nakauchi / Yamanaka, JMA J 2018 Fig. 1. |

No inspected source publishes a journal-required glow recipe or proportion
set. Waddington landscapes and generic branching trees were **not** the
dominant layouts in the inspected Cell / Nature / Science cardiac papers.

## 2. Directed differentiation into cardiovascular cell types

The layout that reads without a caption is a **timed protocol plus a
concentric triple-reporter organoid**, not a Waddington landscape.

- Timeline: **CHIR, then IWR/IWP**, yielding beating TNNT2+ cardiomyocytes.
  Control in Abilez uses only CHIR, FGF2, and IWR.
- Geometry (Abilez et al., Science 2025, Condition 32 time-lapse and day-16
  MIP): **green TNNT2-GFP cardiomyocyte ring**; **CDH5-mOrange endothelial
  cells radiating and branching in orange**; **TAGLN-CFP smooth-muscle
  cells in cyan/blue toward the center**.

Lineage inventory as drawn in that geometry:

- Cardiomyocytes: pulsatile inner ring, TNNT2 / cTnT.
- Endothelium: outward radiation, then branched plexus.
- Smooth muscle: central core.
- Pericytes: PDGFR-β+ wrap around endothelium (see RQ2).
- Fibroblasts / epicardium: not a verified drawable convention in this pass.

## 3. Beating and sarcomeres (illustration, not a micrograph)

To look alive without a cartoon heart:

- **Displacement contours** over milliseconds (Rossi et al., Cell Stem Cell
  2021 Fig. 1A: white and red lines over 10 ms).
- **Rhythmic calcium transients** (Cal-520 AM / light-sheet in Rossi;
  circular beating or wavefront-like Ca2+ in Drakhlis HFOs).
- Vessels **moving in unison** with each contraction (Abilez fig. S4C,
  movie S5).
- Sarcomeres as **cTnT / alpha-actinin / MHC cross-striations** (IF or TEM),
  never a love-heart icon.

Hofbauer et al., Cell 2021 (hollow beating TNNT2 cardioids; Fluo-4) is
widely cited for chamber-cavity visuals but was **not retrieved** here
and is not a standalone claim.

Paywalled Cell/Nature cover pages blocked a complete ten-item open-image
list. The 2013 Cell cover of an iPSC in a red T-shirt (Xu et al.) is a
metaphor, not a morphological template; it was not re-fetched after an
archive URL failed.

## 5. DO / DON’T

**DO**

- Packed, flat, ES-like colonies with large nuclei.
- Circular micropattern as the starting plane (~30 µm thick in Abilez).
- CHIR then IWR timeline.
- Green CM ring / orange EC plexus / cyan SMC core.
- Displacement, calcium wave, and sarcomere striations for “alive.”

**DON’T**

- Generic glowing stem-cell mass.
- Cartoon heart as the cardiomyocyte glyph.
- Waddington landscape or branching tree as the default (not supported by
  the inspected cardiac papers).
- OCT4-green + SOX2-red as a **cover-grade** palette (Cell Stem Cell bans
  red with green). Reporter colors stay in data figures.
- Treat any single palette as a journal-required style bible (none exists
  in the inspected sources).
