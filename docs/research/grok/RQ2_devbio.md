# RQ2 — Visual language of developmental biology, organoids, and gastruloids

**Status: Partial.** Stage sequence and doughnut geometry are verified.
Heart-tube looping, chamber ballooning, epicardium, and trabeculation were
**not** recovered as a four-stage drawable strip in the inspected papers.
Those papers compress to crescent → beating bud/tube.

Anchor paper: Abilez et al., Science 2025 (lab gastruloid vascularization).

## 1. Embryonic heart development (compressed stages)

Inspected drawable sequence (Rossi et al., Cell Stem Cell 2021, aligned to
E7.5–E8.5):

1. **cTnT cardiac crescent** at 144 h (crescent-like domain).
2. Condenses into a **beating bud / linear tube** by 168 h.
3. Cardiac domain sits next to an **anterior gut-like epithelial tube**.
4. The two domains are split by a **CD31+ endocardial-like layer**.
5. First-heart-field (Hcn4-GFP) sits **more anterior** than second-heart-field
   (Tbx1-Cre-RFP), opposite a Brachyury+ posterior pole.

Not verified as a 3–4 icon strip in this pass: looping, ballooning,
epicardial covering, trabeculation. Do not invent those stages as
“canonical” from this packet.

## 2. Organoids and gastruloids that look three-dimensional

Verified geometry is **layered or doughnut-like, not a uniform sphere**.

From Abilez et al. (cVOs) and Zhao & Luo restatement:

- Starting circular micropattern ~**30 µm** thick.
- 3D construct ~**300 µm** in z.
- Pulsatile **inner cardiomyocyte ring**.
- **Orange/magenta CD31 vascular plexus**, lumenized branches **4–40 µm**
  diameter, penetrating the myocardium.
- **PDGFR-β+ pericytes** around endothelium inside TNNT2-GFP CMs.
- Mural / SMC wrap toward the center (TAGLN-CFP).

From Drakhlis et al., Nat Biotechnol 2021 (heart-forming organoids):

- ~**2 mm** construct.
- Inner core + **NKX2.5-eGFP myocardial layer** + outer layer.
- CD31 vessel-like networks in the inner core.
- **Endocardial-like endothelial lining** of the myocardium.

Translucency / cutaway: 3D surface renderings and MIP confocal, not a
glass sphere with a fake glow. Nascent vessels are a **branched lumenized
plexus**, not a halo.

## 3. Development as a process in one static frame

Inspected papers use:

- **Timed protocol strips** (CHIR day 0, IWR day 3, then vascular factors).
- **Anatomical stage strips** (crescent → bud aligned to E7.5–E8.5).
- Concentric rings as the “already happened” product of that timeline.

Not found as the dominant device: spiral timelines, morphing sequences,
Waddington landscapes.

For a single hero frame: put the **crescent → bud** as a faint background
plane and the **doughnut organoid** as the focal product (see RQ7).

## 5. DO / DON’T

**DO**

- Layered doughnut or myocardial shell with a real lumenized plexus.
- Name vessel scale (4–40 µm) so branches look capillary, not cartoon vines.
- Show polarity (anterior crescent/bud vs posterior pole).
- Stack depth: flat ~30 µm seed → ~300 µm organoid (or ~2 mm HFO).
- Use MIP / surface-render language (translucent layers, not a plastic ball).

**DON’T**

- Uniform glowing sphere as “the organoid.”
- Skip lumina (unlumenized scribbles read as decoration).
- Center a cartoon heart in the doughnut hole.
- Pair red with green on a cover-grade frame (CD31-magenta is figure grammar;
  Cell Stem Cell cover rules forbid red+green).
- Reproduce a paper figure as the website hero if the goal is cover grammar.
