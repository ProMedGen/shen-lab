# RQ6 — Visual language of cardio-oncology

**Status: Partial.** Sequence and balance are verified. Dual-action
heart-versus-tumor glyphs, shield conventions, and a ten-item Circulation /
JACC / Cancer Cell cover catalog were not recovered as cover art. Local
`cardio-oncology.jpg` is stock left-heart / right-tumor, not a 2024–2026
journal cover.

## 1. Element inventory: drug that fights tumor but threatens the heart

Inspected 2024–2025 expert art does **not** use a single dual-action
heart-versus-tumor glyph.

Basic Research in Cardiology 2025 (anthracycline review):

- **Left panel:** toxicity mechanisms.
- **Right panel:** cardioprotective therapies that target those mechanisms.
- Then a **balance**: mild/reversible injury (“permissive cardiotoxicity”)
  versus irreversible structural or functional damage.

JACC: CardioOncology, November 2024, expert-panel Central Illustration:

Three **sequential strategies**, not one icon:

1. **Cardioprotection** — minimize cardiovascular toxicity from cancer
   treatment.
2. **Surveillance** — ongoing monitoring during therapy.
3. **Permissive cardiotoxicity** — management that **allows continuation**
   of cardiotoxic cancer treatment.

## 2. How toxicity is stylized

Verified wording: spectrum from mild/reversible injury to
moderate/severe **irreversible structural or functional impairment**.
The damaged portion of the heart sits on one side of a **balance**.

Not verified in this pass: fraying sarcomeres, mitochondrial damage, or
dark lesions as homepage-safe stylization. Do not invent gruesome
myocardium from this packet. If sarcomere language is needed, borrow RQ1
cross-striations and show them **disrupted on one side of the balance
only**.

## 3. How protection / rescue is stylized

Verified: a **right-hand therapy panel** and the “acceptable injury”
side of the balance. Not verified: shield conventions, small-molecule
glow, restoration bloom. Those read as pharma-ad tropes and were not
found as required journal grammar here.

Local site art (`cardio-oncology.jpg`) is a 3D still of left heart / right
tumor **without** a protection or dual-action overlay. Do not treat it as
a model.

## 5. DO / DON’T

**DO**

- Three-beat sequence: protect, watch, permit.
- Mechanism panel versus therapy panel.
- A balance of reversible vs irreversible injury.
- Keep this as a **thin bottom bar** on the hero (RQ7 #3), not the focal
  metaphor.

**DON’T**

- Heart punching a tumor, or tumor punching a heart.
- Pharma shield + glow restoration as the only story.
- Gruesome myocardium on a lab homepage (not supported, and not needed).
- Promote cardio-oncology to the center of the 16:9 frame; identity is the
  doughnut (RQ7 #1).
