# Research result: cardiovascular-lab hero visual language

**Status: Partial**

Human iPSC-to-heart illustration for a cardiovascular-lab hero is a
**layered doughnut, not a cartoon heart**: packed flat colonies become a
green TNNT2 cardiomyocyte ring, orange branching endothelium, and
cyan/blue central smooth muscle, with beating shown as displacement,
calcium, and sarcomere striations. CRISPR is a bilobed protein or
parallel CRISPRi/a arms on a left-to-right screen, not scissors;
virtual-cell ML is a cell on a circuit or a field of oval tokens in a
five-stage predict-measure loop; cardio-oncology is sequential
cardioprotection, surveillance, and permissive toxicity, not a
heart-versus-tumor glyph. Cover-grade art is one narrative metaphor on a
dark 16:9 field (no red-with-green pairing, no glowing blob); generation
prompts are narrative and front-weighted at native 16:9 / 2K, with camera
and depth of field named.

### iPSC identity and directed cardiogenesis

Draw human iPSCs as tightly packed, **flat ES-like colonies**: large
nuclei, scant cytoplasm, circular confluent micropatterns stained OCT4
(green), SOX2 (red), and DAPI (blue). Reprogramming is OSKM converting a
somatic cell into an iPSC that can become any cell type under proper
culture, not a generic glowing stem-cell mass. [S1]

Directed cardiovascular differentiation is a **CHIR then IWR/IWP
timeline** that yields beating TNNT2+ cardiomyocytes. The drawable
geometry is concentric and triple-reporter: a **green TNNT2-GFP
cardiomyocyte ring**, **CDH5-mOrange endothelial cells radiating and
branching in orange**, and **TAGLN-CFP smooth-muscle cells in cyan/blue
toward the center** (control differentiation uses only CHIR, FGF2, and
IWR). [S2]

Beating is displacement contours over milliseconds, rhythmic calcium
transients, and vessels moving in unison with each contraction.
Sarcomeres are cTnT / alpha-actinin (or MHC) **cross-striations** (TEM
or immunofluorescence), never a cartoon heart icon. [S3]

### Heart morphogenesis and vascularized organoid geometry

Compress embryonic cardiogenesis to a short anterior sequence: a **cTnT
cardiac crescent** that condenses into a **beating bud/tube**, sitting
next to an anterior gut-like tube and separated by a **CD31+
endocardial-like layer**. First-heart-field cells sit more anterior than
second-heart-field cells, opposite a Brachyury+ posterior pole. [S4]

Vascularized cardiac organoids and gastruloids are **layered or
doughnut-like, not uniform spheres**: a myocardial shell or pulsatile
inner cardiomyocyte ring, mural wrapping, and a branched lumenized CD31
vascular plexus (**about 4–40 µm diameter**) that penetrates the
cardiomyocytes, with PDGFR-β+ pericytes around the endothelium. Starting
circular micropatterns are ~30 µm thick; the 3D construct reaches ~300 µm
in z; heart-forming organoids are ~2 mm with an inner core, NKX2.5-eGFP
myocardial layer, outer layer, and an endocardial-like endothelial lining
of the myocardium. [S5]

### CRISPR without scissors

In current Cell structure graphics, Cas9 is a **bilobed protein (REC and
NUC)** wrapping a **T-shaped sgRNA:DNA heteroduplex** in a positively
charged groove, shown as ribbon, surface, or electrostatic maps, not a
scissors glyph. [S7]

Dated public education art still names Cas9 a locate-then-cut-then-repair
“cutting device”; helix-plus-scissors remains on older lab theme art,
while the lab’s own CRISPR pipeline already seats a **dCas9-like protein
on a CRISPRi/a DNA loop**. Use the latter language for a new hero. [S8]

Pooled CRISPR-screen schematics in Cell and Cell Stem Cell are
**left-to-right process flows**: oligo/sgRNA library → low-MOI infection
so one guide per cell → selection or toxin → harvest of survivors → NGS
of sgRNA barcodes. Enrichment and depletion are the surviving-cell
stream; scatter plots of hits stay in separate data panels, not inside
the workflow drawing. [S9]

Bidirectional CRISPRi/a is **two parallel dCas9 fusion arms**, KRAB
repressor versus VP64 / SunTag / VPR activator, with anti-correlated
phenotypes (CRISPRa guides peak about −400 to −50 bp of the TSS; CRISPRi
just downstream). Hit graphics are pathway circles (protective versus
sensitizing), not scissors and not a volcano inside the schematic. [S10]

When Perturb-seq papers dramatize thousands of single-cell perturbations,
**labeled embedding / UMAP-style point clouds and heatmaps** are data
figures; graphical abstracts are unlabeled workflow cartoons. A Science
CRISPR special-issue cover used a **photo of an edited tomato seedling**,
not a Cas9 cartoon. [S11]

### Virtual cells and machine-learning metaphor

Science’s virtual-cell feature is a **biological cell centered on a
circuit board**, not a three-layer perceptron and not a blue brain. [S12]

Nature’s virtual-cell opener is a **geometric field of oval cell tokens
with square cores**, split dark/light, with a pointing finger, under the
line “CAN BIOLOGY MOVE INTO THE MATRIX?” [S13]

Lab-in-the-loop is a **five-stage closed cycle**: the virtual cell
predicts a perturbation response; it explains the prediction
mechanistically; agents design falsifying experiments; real labs execute
them; the model updates when a hypothesis is falsified. Virtual cells are
drawn as falsifiable theories of cellular biology, not as a static
neural-net glyph. [S14]

### Cardio-oncology as sequence and balance

Anthracycline cardio-oncology is a **left panel of toxicity mechanisms
versus a right panel of cardioprotective therapies**, then a **balance**:
mild/reversible “permissive cardiotoxicity” on one side, irreversible
structural or functional damage on the other. [S15]

Expert-panel care is **three sequential strategies**, cardioprotection,
surveillance, permissive cardiotoxicity, not a single dual-action
heart-versus-tumor glyph. Cardioprotection minimizes toxicity;
surveillance is ongoing monitoring during therapy; permissive
cardiotoxicity is the management that allows continuation of cardiotoxic
cancer treatment. [S16]

### Cover grammar versus figure grammar

Journal **covers** are artistic, non-figure images with a specific
narrative metaphor (neuroblasts as flying birds and V-SVZ as a phoenix; a
corpus-callosum organoid as a bridge between metaphorical brain
hemispheres / city skylines). Cell Stem Cell forbids pairing **red with
green**, forbids reproducing a paper figure as the cover, and **bans
generative-AI cover art**. Data figures may use OCT4 green / SOX2 red; a
website hero that wants cover-grade grammar should keep the metaphor and
drop the red–green pairing, the blob, and the scissors. [S6]

### Three geometric 16:9 hero blueprints (ranked)

On a **dark 16:9** field, rank by how completely the packet’s own shapes
carry the lab story without banned cover tropes (cartoon heart, uniform
sphere, glowing blob, scissors, perceptron/brain, red-with-green,
heart-versus-tumor).

This ranking is **design synthesis from verified shapes**, not a journal
finding. No inspected 2024–2026 source ranks three geometrically
specified composition blueprints for a lab-website hero.

**1 — Concentric doughnut (use this).** Full-frame shallow-DOF organoid:
pulsatile inner **green TNNT2 ring**, **orange** endothelial radiation
and 4–40 µm lumenized branches penetrating the myocardium, **cyan/blue**
central SMCs and mural wrap, ~300 µm of stacked depth from a flat ~30 µm
circular micropattern. Background plane: faint anterior **crescent →
beating bud** beside a gut-like tube, split by a thin CD31 endocardial
sheet; FHF anterior, SHF behind. Beating = displacement ticks and a
calcium wave, plus a short cTnT striation inset, not a heart icon. This
is the only geometry that is simultaneously iPSC, morphogenesis, and
vascularized myocardium. [S2]

**2 — Split dark/light token field plus silicon cell.** Left/dark: oval
cell tokens with square cores in a geometric lattice; right/light: one
biological cell seated on circuit traces. A hand or pointer selects one
token; that token expands into the doughnut of rank 1. Use for a
virtual-cell / multiomic reading of the same biology, not as a substitute
for the organoid. [S13]

**3 — Closed pentagon with a three-beat care bar (secondary strip only).**
A five-node cycle on the right third (predict → explain → agents design →
wet lab → update) and a left-to-right CRISPRi/a double arm (KRAB vs VPR)
feeding a surviving-cell stream into NGS barcodes. A thin bottom bar
sequences cardioprotection → surveillance → permissive-toxicity balance.
Do not promote this schematic to the hero focal plane; it is process, not
identity. [S14]

### Prompt craft for a 2K widescreen hero

Build depth in **sequential planes**: background, then foreground, then
focal object; name camera, lens, and DOF (including low-angle with
shallow depth of field, f/1.8). [S17]

Google’s formula is a narrative brief, not a keyword list: **[Subject] +
[Action] + [Location/context] + [Composition] + [Style]**. Anchor style
in editorial/photographic nouns (magazine film grain, named lighting,
materials). Use **positive framing** (describe the empty street, not “no
cars”). [S18]

OpenAI’s order is **background/scene → subject → key details →
constraints**, in labeled segments, with framing, lighting, and DOF
named. Treat “no extra text” and “no watermark” as explicit constraints.
Its scientific samples are **labeled diagrams and charts**, not text-free
covers, so a text-free hero is a cover-style brief, not that diagram
template. [S19]

**2K widescreen is first-class:** Gemini 3.1 Flash Image (Nano Banana 2)
takes `aspect_ratio` 16:9 and `image_size` 2K (also 1K/4K); gpt-image-2’s
recommended reliability ceiling is **2560×1440**, with larger outputs
experimental. [S20]

Adobe Firefly exposes both stacks. Prompt anatomy is six **front-weighted**
layers: subject, setting, style, composition (including DOF), lighting,
model tags. Firefly markets GPT Image 2 for **infographics with readable
in-image text** (up to six references); Gemini/Nano Banana is the better
seat for complex multi-element scenes. A text-free dark hero should not
use the infographic path. [S21]

Unwanted type: quote and pre-generate any wanted copy (Google), or forbid
extras with “no extra text / no watermark” (OpenAI). Both still fail on
misspellings, complex typography, and precise placement, so keep the hero
**type-free** and add labels in post. [S22]

**DO:** packed flat iPSC colonies; CHIR→IWR timeline; green ring / orange
plexus / cyan core; crescent→bud; 4–40 µm lumina; sarcomere striations
and calcium; bilobed Cas9 or parallel KRAB/VPR arms; left-to-right
surviving-cell screen; cell-on-circuit or oval tokens; five-stage loop;
three-step cardio-oncology bar; one metaphor; 16:9 at 2K; named lens and
f/1.8.

**DON’T:** cartoon heart; uniform sphere; glowing blob; red with green on
cover-grade frames; scissors; volcano-in-schematic; heart-versus-tumor;
perceptron or blue brain; keyword-only prompts; in-image titles or
watermarks; generative-AI if the destination is a Cell Stem Cell cover.
